Marketing Campaign ROI & Conversion Analysis

Which campaigns deserve
next quarter's budget?

A full-funnel read on 40 campaigns across six channels — Facebook, Google, Email, Instagram, TikTok and Affiliate — from impression to purchase, plus a statistically rigorous read on a live landing-page experiment.

Headline finding — Email returns $22.02 in revenue for every $1 spent (2,102% ROI), while Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are currently unprofitable (ROAS below 1.0). Reallocating even 30% of Meta/TikTok spend into Email and Google could lift blended portfolio ROAS materially without adding budget.

Facebook and Instagram command the largest impression volumes, but capital efficiency — CAC, ROAS, ROI — tells a very different story.

Cut candidate — TikTok spent $10,485 to generate $1,547 in revenue (ROAS 0.15, CAC $308 per purchase). Even generously assuming brand-awareness value not captured in direct response, this is the clearest reallocation candidate in the portfolio.
04 — Trends Over Time

Revenue, profit, and month-over-month growth

Monthly revenue and profit with a running cumulative total and MoM % growth — the volatility across Q1–Q2 tracks directly with which channels were active each month.

Reading the swings — June's +217% MoM spike and September's +274% rebound both coincide with concentrated Email campaign launches; the August and December troughs coincide with gaps in the Email calendar. Revenue volatility here is a scheduling artifact, not demand volatility — an argument for a steadier, always-on Email cadence.
05 — Geography × Device × Age

Where performance concentrates

Cross-cut of ROAS by region, device focus, and age group. Green rows are compounding well; red rows are compounding losses.

Best-fit audience — North America + Tablet + Age 35–44 delivers 22.75x ROAS — the single strongest segment in the dataset — while Europe/APAC desktop campaigns targeting 45–54 consistently underperform (ROAS < 1.5). Segment-level budget shifting, not just channel-level, is leaving money on the table.
